Our online retail company wants to increase sales, improve customer retention, and optimise marketing strategies by analysing historical customer transaction data. The company has collected detailed information about customer demographics, product purchases, order details, and payment behaviour.

The dataset includes the following columns: Customer ID, Age, Gender, Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount, Location, Size, Colour, Season, Review Rating, Subscription Status, Shipping Type, Discount Applied, Previous Purchase, and Payment Method.

The business problem is to extract meaningful insights from this dataset to answer questions such as:

Who are the high-value customers?
Which products and categories generate the most revenue, and what are the popular size/colour preferences?
How do seasonality impact sales performance?
How do subscription status, payment method, and shipping type influence purchase behaviour and revenue?
What is the relationship between customer satisfaction (review ratings) and repeat purchases?
